Quality Control

The Inkit 5-Step Precision Engineering Process

We don't simply drill holes and squirt ink. Every cartridge undergoes a cleanroom industrial remanufacturing workflow.

1

12-Point Inspection

Electrical circuit conductivity checks, roller surface integrity testing, and microchip health analysis.

2

Ultrasonic De-clog

Sonic bath dissolves dried ink crystals from thermal nozzles; high-pressure vacuum evacuates residual waste powder.

3

Precision Fill

Calibrated micro-gram digital scales measure OEM volume of German-engineered chemically matched ink or toner.

4

Chip Coding

Installation of brand new smart microchips to ensure full OEM printer handshake and real-time level reporting.

5

Test Page QC

Diagnostic test print of high-density black blocks and CMYK color gradients. Certified sample is enclosed with every order.
