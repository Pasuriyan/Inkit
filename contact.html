Care Guide

Drop-Off & Transportation Instructions

Follow these quick tips to prevent ink leakage or drum scratching when bringing your cartridges to our shop.

Place each empty ink cartridge upright inside a small sealed ziplock bag with a lightly damp paper towel. Never touch the copper-colored electronic contact ribbon, and never stick adhesive tape directly over the printhead nozzles, as the adhesive residue can permanently block microscopic ink channels.

Laser toner cartridges contain a sensitive organic photo-conductor (OPC) drum coated in light-reactive green or turquoise chemicals. Keep the protective plastic drum shutter closed and place the cartridge in a dark bag or box during transit. Direct sunlight for more than 5 minutes can permanently burn the drum coating.

Original OEM laser toner cartridges can typically be remanufactured and refilled between 4 to 7 cycles, provided the internal gears and wiper blades are refreshed. Integrated inkjet cartridges can usually undergo 3 to 5 vacuum refill cycles before internal sponge degradation occurs.
